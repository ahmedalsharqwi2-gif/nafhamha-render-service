# Niche strategy: نفهمها

Goal: each day pick a topic with real search demand, a competition gap, and channel fit, then package it with an honest title and hook. Nobody can guarantee views. The system raises the odds, measures results and adjusts. It never promises numbers.

Profile: `config/niche_profile.json` (audience, pillars, banned topics, tone, question patterns). Weights: `config/selection_weights.json`.

Pillars: إزاي بتشتغل الأشياء اليومية (تكنولوجيا وأجهزة), اقتصاد وفلوس مبسط (مفاهيم عامة فقط), علوم يومية, أسباب اجتماعية وتاريخية مبسطة, مصطلحات متداولة في الأخبار. Search intent: يعني إيه…، إزاي…، ليه…، الفرق بين….

Channel rules: health/legal/investment: general explanation from an official source with no personal recommendations, otherwise exclude.

Score (0-100) = demand 30 + gap/outliers 25 + rising trend 10 + channel fit 15 + visual filmability 10 + hook 10, minus penalties for saturation and risk. Unknown components are excluded and rescaled, and the score is marked `degraded`. Nothing is estimated from memory.

Mix: bank 75% evergreen / 25% trending. A trending item must map to a pillar (whole-word match) or it is dropped as off-niche. Candidates must pass 60-day no-repeat and the pillar rule (same pillar at most twice in a row), plus the existing safety and source checks downstream.

Audit trail: `state/niche/<date>.json` holds the winner, the full component breakdown, top 10, warnings and secret status. `<date>.packaging.json` holds titles, hook, CTA, description, hashtags, AI disclosure and the title-promise check.

Topic bank: `topic_bank/*.json`, at least 150 ideas, generated by Gemini in the workflow (`node niche_intel/run.mjs bank-seed`), all `pending` until demand data validates them. Pending ideas are only used when no validated idea exists and are flagged.

This feature publishes nothing and does not touch `publish_enabled`.
